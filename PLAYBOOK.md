# 30-day launch playbook (honest)

Goal printed in the brief: **$1,000 this month**. That number is a planning target, not a promise. New domains plus India display RPM plus AdSense review time make organic $1k in the remaining days of September 2026 extremely unlikely. This playbook is how the portfolio can compound toward that run-rate.

## Why the math is hard

- India jobs / exam / scheme traffic often lands around **$0.50–$3 page RPM**.
- $1,000 at $2 RPM is **500,000 pageviews**.
- A new domain needs indexing, then rankings, then an approved AdSense account.
- Google forbids bought PTC / autosurf / incentivized clicks. Those packages will not be used.

HisabBox (finance calculators) can earn a higher RPM than NaukriSetu if it also attracts Gulf / English EMI-SIP queries. That is why the five sites are not five clones of the same jobs list.

## Week 1 — ship and apply

1. Buy five domains. Keep WHOIS and Google accounts consistent (one publisher, one AdSense account).
2. Deploy this repo. Confirm HTTPS, mobile layout, About / Privacy / Contact / Disclaimer.
3. Search Console + sitemap for each host.
4. Do **not** apply to AdSense on an empty shell. Wait until each host has the seeded guides + listing pages live.
5. Replace `ca-pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX` only after approval.

## Week 2 — distribution that is allowed

- One Telegram channel per jobs + exam site. Post last-date cards that link to *your* briefing, then the official URL.
- WhatsApp status pages are fine; WhatsApp groups that beg for ad clicks are not.
- ParikshaDaily daily quiz at 07:00 IST. Same URL every day so Direct traffic builds.
- HisabBox: index the English titles (“EMI calculator India”, “SIP calculator 12 percent”) as well as Hindi search.

## Week 3 — refresh winners

- Update last dates and corrigenda. Fresh `updatedAt` on pages that already have impressions.
- Internally link job pages → FormKitIN photo/age and HisabBox in-hand salary. Session RPM beats page RPM.
- Kill any thin page that is only a title and a button.

## Week 4 — measure before you touch ads

- Segment pageviews by site and country.
- Keep Auto ads off the header, footer and calculator result well.
- One in-article + one mobile anchor is enough until you have a month of clean data.

## Content cadence after launch

| Site | Daily | Weekly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NaukriSetu | 3–5 new briefings from official PDFs | 1 explainer |
| YojanaPath | 1 status / document note | 1 scheme dossier |
| ParikshaDaily | 1 quiz | 1 cutoff / syllabus refresh |
| HisabBox | — | 1 calculator tweak or tax note |
| FormKitIN | — | 1 notice-specific photo spec |

Never scrape a competitor’s HTML. Write the briefing from the official PDF.

## Policy lines we will not cross

- No “click our ads to support us”.
- No fake “Notification Soon” doorway pages.
- No claiming to be SSC / IBPS / a ministry.
- No uploading user photographs to our server.
- One AdSense account. Do not open a second account if the first is limited.
